# Technology Research Rubric

## Currency

- The report has an explicit as-of timestamp.
- Version and release dates are checked against current primary sources.
- Shipped, preview, announced, deprecated, and removed behavior are not conflated.

## Evidence

- Material claims have direct links near the claim.
- Sources directly support the wording used.
- Facts and inference are distinguishable.
- Contradictions, inaccessible sources, and unknowns are visible.
- Search-result pages and unsourced snippets are not citations.

## Relevance

- The user's current baseline and constraints are explicit.
- Changes are mapped to concrete architecture, roadmap, security, cost, or operating implications.
- Popularity alone does not drive the recommendation.
- Experiments are proposed only for material unresolved questions.

## Decision

- The recommendation names its confidence and reversal conditions.
- Adoption language matches the evidence.
- Migration effort and reversibility are considered.

Return `PASS`, `REVISE`, or `BLOCKED` and list only corrections that could change a decision or the report's trustworthiness.
