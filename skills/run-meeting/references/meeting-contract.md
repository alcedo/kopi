# Meeting Contract

## Before scheduling

- Purpose and decision are explicit.
- Required participants are resolved to the correct identities.
- Roles justify attendance.
- Date range, duration, time zones, working hours, and availability are checked.
- An equivalent event does not already exist.
- Agenda, location, conferencing, and pre-read are ready or clearly marked pending.
- The user's wording authorizes the requested calendar write.

## After scheduling or updating

- Read the event from the calendar rather than trusting the create response alone.
- Verify event identifier, calendar, title, start, end, time zone, participants, recurrence, conferencing, links, and status.
- Report notifications or changes that may affect attendees.
- Treat an uncertain result as unresolved. Search before retrying.

## After the meeting

- Decisions include owner, rationale, date, and review condition where relevant.
- Dissent and unresolved questions remain visible.
- Actions have one owner, outcome, due date or missing-date flag, and destination when tracked.
- Notes distinguish statements made in the meeting from later inference.

## Verdict

Use `READY`, `CREATED`, `UPDATED`, `CANCELLED`, `CLOSED`, `BLOCKED`, or `UNCERTAIN`. Never report a calendar mutation as complete without reading back the actual event.
