## Task

Identify lessons that could change a future decision in similar project or architecture work. Trace each proposal to evidence in the supplied record. Distinguish the root breakdown from downstream inconvenience.

Prefer corrections that make the right behavior easier to execute or mechanically check. Do not turn a personal preference, isolated event, or already-covered rule into new universal guidance.

## Return

For each proposal:

- Situation that triggers it.
- Evidence and confidence.
- Future decision or behavior changed.
- Narrow owning artifact.
- Structural control or prose edit.
- Evaluation scenario.
- Cost or downside.

Also return rejected ideas and why they should not become durable rules.
