# Performance Review Report Contract

## Inputs and outputs

Draft from the evidence map, manager observations, and selected idea IDs. Keep a private selection record with included, combined, qualified, deferred, and excluded ideas plus concise reasons. Give useful editorial rationale, evidence, and alternatives rather than claiming to expose hidden internal reasoning.

Produce two separate, complete outputs. Use the requested format; for files, create separate employee and manager files. Without a file request, use two clearly labeled, independently copyable text artifacts. Do not put confidential notes in an appendix to the employee report.

## Employee report

Use the supplied template if present; otherwise:

1. **Overview:** A balanced assessment of the review period, recurring themes, strongest contributions, and consequential development needs. Match emphasis and certainty to the evidence. Acknowledge materially mixed feedback without attribution.
2. **Strengths:** Theme by theme, describe the behavior, contribution or impact, and supporting examples that can be shared safely. Be specific without inventing metrics, outcomes, or sole ownership of team achievements.
3. **Areas for improvement:** Theme by theme, explain the observed behavior, impact or limitation, and a practical next step. Distinguish proposed actions and success indicators from agreed commitments. Do not invent deadlines or targets. Where evidence does not support an improvement theme, say so rather than creating one for symmetry.

Keep the report substantive: removing reviewer attribution should not remove the meaningful feedback. Include confidence limits necessary to understand the assessment, but put evidence collection requests and detailed uncertainty analysis in manager notes.

### Protect reviewer identities

Remove reviewer names, source IDs, source links, reviewer tables, and quotes attributable by distinctive wording. Check indirect clues: unique roles, one-to-one interactions, exact dates, small-group counts, projects or incident details known to only a few participants. Merely replacing a name with “a peer” does not make the passage anonymous.

Paraphrase or generalize identifying context while preserving the behavior and supported impact. Do not manufacture a replacement incident, broaden one event into a pattern, or conceal uncertainty to make the wording smoother. If a useful example cannot be de-identified, retain the detail privately and use a supported behavior-level statement in the employee copy. A fact independently present in the person's own reflection may be usable, but do not link it to a confidential criticism in a way that exposes its author.

For files, also check comments, tracked changes, notes, hyperlinks, attachments, and metadata for reviewer information. Do not claim guaranteed anonymity: record residual inference risks privately for the manager's contextual check. A label saying “confidential” is not access control; keep private content out of any employee-shared destination.

## Confidential manager report

Include the complete assessment, not just a pointer to the employee copy, plus:

- Role, review scope, source inventory and coverage limitations.
- Reviewer-by-reviewer summaries and the per-theme evidence tables from [evidence synthesis](evidence-synthesis.md), with source locators and attribution.
- Self-reflection alignment, disagreement, repeated accounts of the same event, and limits on recurrence claims.
- Manager observations and judgment with separate provenance.
- Numbered selection record, chosen emphasis, and reasons for qualifying, deferring, combining, or excluding ideas.
- Gaps for the manager to supplement, material unresolved questions, proposed follow-ups, and confidentiality transformations or remaining inference risks.

Keep the private record complete without multiplying identical tables. Link internally to evidence rows when that is clearer than repeating them.

## Verification before return

- Every consequential assessment maps to supplied evidence or a labeled manager observation; severity, attribution, scope, and numerical claims are supported.
- The user's selected ideas are included or their accurate qualification is explained privately. IDs retain their meaning. Proposed actions are labeled and factual counterevidence has not been erased.
- Both reports contain a full assessment with the same supported meaning and uncertainty. Neither assigns an invented rating or retroactive goal.
- Inspect the employee artifact separately, including its non-body content when applicable, for direct and indirect reviewer identification. Keep all private tables and source trails in the manager copy.
- Read back generated files and follow the host's document verification requirements. Report any incomplete checks precisely. Do not share either report or change external permissions merely to complete a draft.
