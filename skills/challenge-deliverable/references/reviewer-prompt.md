## Task

Review the artifact through the assigned lens. Try to falsify its consequential claims and identify omissions that could change the intended decision. Stay inside the artifact's purpose and supplied constraints.

Do not edit the artifact. Do not reward verbosity. Do not file style preferences, speculative future problems, or gaps outside the intended scope.

## Finding format

- Location.
- Finding.
- Evidence.
- Decision or trust impact.
- Suggested correction, only when it is clear.
- Confidence.

Return `NO FINDINGS` when the lens surfaces nothing consequential.
