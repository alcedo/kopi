# Review Lenses

Choose only what applies.

## Evidence and epistemics

Unsupported claims, stale sources, contradictory evidence, hidden inference, false precision, and confidence stronger than the record.

## Decision and strategy

Unclear decision, weak option comparison, missing reversal conditions, incentives, opportunity cost, and recommendations that do not follow from evidence.

## Architecture and operations

Ownership, boundaries, data flow, trust, security, failure, recovery, operability, migration, performance, cost, and blast radius.

## Delivery and portfolio

Unowned actions, impossible sequencing, hidden dependencies, missing milestones, stale status, unmitigated risk, and plans with no observable completion state.

## Data and analysis

Metric definitions, population, missingness, joins, mix shifts, sensitivity, causal overreach, misleading charts, and unreproducible calculations.

## Audience and narrative

Mismatch between audience and depth, unclear takeaway, buried ask, inconsistent terms, unsupported confidence, excessive detail, and visuals that obscure the message.

## Meeting effectiveness

No decision owner, unnecessary attendees, information presented synchronously without discussion need, agenda that cannot fit, missing pre-read, and actions without owners.
