# Gather Evidence

**Invoked by any playbook that makes a claim.** It owns provenance: every claim traceable to a dated, resolvable source with confidence and freshness labelled.

1. Turn the frame's decision into the specific questions evidence must answer. Collect against those questions only.
2. Rank sources by authority for the question at hand. Live systems and primary records beat summaries. Summaries beat recollection. Prior conversation and old reports are history, not current state.
3. Record each finding as an evidence row from the [artifact model](../references/artifact-model.md): claim, source, source date, retrieved at, confidence, freshness. A claim with no resolvable source is inference and gets labelled as inference.
4. Keep four categories apart: what a source states, what it plans, how others interpret it, and what you inferred. Never merge them into one voice.
5. Reconcile conflicts instead of picking a quiet winner. Report both sources, their dates, and which is authoritative for this decision. Leave a genuine contradiction open.
6. Name what you could not get. Unavailable, access-denied, and stale evidence are findings. A gap stated is usable. A gap smoothed over corrupts everything built on it.
7. Hand the rows to the owning playbook. Keep the raw sweep out of the final artifact and keep the source pointers in.

Stop collecting when the next source would not change the recommendation.
