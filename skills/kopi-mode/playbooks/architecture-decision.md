# Architecture Decision Playbook

Use `decide-architecture`.

1. Ground the current system, ownership, constraints, data flows, and historical rationale.
2. Write the caller, operator, or stakeholder experience before component structure. Settle an open shape by observation with [sketch options](sketch-options.md).
3. Generate at least two structurally different options for consequential decisions, under [compare options](compare-options.md).
4. Compare boundaries, security, operability, migration, cost, failure modes, blast radius, and reversibility.
5. Select a recommendation with explicit tradeoffs and unresolved risks.
6. Produce a decision record and diagrams appropriate to the audience. Do not implement unless separately requested.

If new evidence invalidates the shape, redesign rather than adding exceptions to preserve it.
