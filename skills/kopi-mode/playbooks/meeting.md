# Meeting Playbook

Use `run-meeting`.

1. Decide whether the request is to prepare, schedule, update, or close a meeting.
2. Define the purpose and decision needed. Resolve participants, duration, date range, time zones, availability, and supporting material.
3. Draft the agenda and pre-read before scheduling when they affect invite content.
4. Apply the external-write authority test. Prevent duplicates, perform the requested calendar action, and read back the resulting event.
5. After the meeting, capture decisions, dissent, actions, owners, and dates. Update trackers or send notes only when requested. Open actions and unresolved decisions pass to [follow through](follow-through.md).

Do not create a meeting when an asynchronous decision or existing forum clearly satisfies the purpose better. Recommend that alternative and explain why.
