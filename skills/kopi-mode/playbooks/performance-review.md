# Performance Review Playbook

Use [write-performance-review](../../write-performance-review/SKILL.md).

1. Obtain the role and supplied 360 feedback and self-reflection. Goals are optional.
2. Synthesize the evidence first per [gather evidence](gather-evidence.md), preserving source identity privately, observation scope, disagreements, and gaps.
3. Present private reviewer tables and stable numbered themes with a recommended emphasis.
4. Invite the manager's observations and selection before drafting, unless choosing and finalizing was explicitly delegated. Record additions with separate provenance.
5. Produce a complete employee report with reviewer identities protected, and a separate complete manager report with detailed evidence and working notes.
6. Verify evidence, selected ideas, and confidentiality. Keep ambiguous material conclusions open and proceed with supported ones.

A narrow edit to an agreed review does not restart intake. Drafting does not authorize sharing, contacting reviewers, or updating HR records.
