# Follow Through

**Invoked when an artifact has shipped but the outcome depends on other people acting.** It owns the open items: decisions awaiting an owner, actions awaiting a date, approvals awaiting a person.

1. Declare the mode before touching anything. Check reports current state once and stops. Chase drives open items toward closure. Threads only answers the specific comments raised and changes nothing else. Undeclared defaults to check.
2. List the open items with one accountable owner and one next action each. An item with no owner is the first thing to report, not something to assign yourself.
3. Work the blocking item first. The item that gates the others is the only one that matters until it clears. Do not tidy downstream items while the blocker stands.
4. Read current state from the authoritative system, never from the artifact that requested the change. A tracker row, calendar event, or document is truth. Your own earlier report is not.
5. Separate waiting from blocked. Waiting has an owner and a date. Blocked has a missing decision, input, or authority. Escalate a blocked item by naming what is missing and who can supply it.
6. Keep every update inside authority you already hold. Reminders, drafts, and status reports proceed. Reassignment, cancellation, date changes, and messages to people take the gate in [permissions and writes](../references/permissions-and-writes.md).
7. Report the frontier: what closed, what waits on whom by when, what is blocked and on what, and what needs the user's decision. Re-check only where state can have changed.

Chasing is not progress. Stop when the remaining items sit outside your authority, and say who owns each one.
