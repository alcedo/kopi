# Learning Capture Playbook

Use `capture-learning`.

1. Reconstruct what happened from the work trail and evidence.
2. Separate a repeated or generalizable failure from a one-off event.
3. Identify the future decision that should change.
4. Prefer a required field, template, validator, automation, ownership rule, or routing condition over another paragraph of guidance.
5. Propose the narrowest durable change and its evaluation scenario.
6. Get approval before editing shared skills or workflows. Record rejected and deferred proposals.
