# Compare Writing Ideas

Use when a writing workflow needs the user's choice of themes or framing before drafting. This is a reusable component, not a requirement for every writing task. A single agent is sufficient; do not imply independent judging or model consensus when it did not happen.

## Contract

**Inputs:** Audience, purpose, supported claims with source pointers and limits, relevant constraints, and selection mode: ask the user or choose and finish when explicitly delegated.

**Outputs:** A stable numbered idea table and recommendation; after selection, a record of included, combined, qualified, deferred, and rejected ideas for the owning workflow to turn into a coherent artifact.

The owning workflow supplies the evidence, confidentiality rules, final format, and verification. This component does not research new facts, authorize writes, or require complete competing drafts.

## Compare

Offer meaningfully different themes, emphases, or framing choices. Keep the underlying facts consistent. Use as many rows as useful; do not pad the table or impose a fixed count. Distinguish a supported assessment from a proposed future action.

| Idea ID | Proposed theme or framing | Evidence and limitations | Benefit / tradeoff | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Recommend a combination with concise reasons. Identify mutually incompatible choices and overlapping ideas that should merge. Keep source-sensitive material in the audience authorized by the owning workflow.

Invite the user to select by ID, accept the recommendation, add context, or combine wording. In ask mode, stop before final drafting; do not simulate the user's selection. In explicitly delegated mode, record the choice and proceed.

## Carry selection forward

Keep IDs stable through revisions. New ideas get new IDs; removed ideas retain their disposition instead of having their numbers recycled. An accepted recommendation selects the recommended IDs without another approval step.

Interpret ordinary selection instructions using the current table. Ask only when a reference is ambiguous or incompatible choices cannot be reconciled. Incorporate new evidence without disguising its source. If it materially changes a selected idea, show that change; do not silently reuse its ID for a different meaning.

Return the selection record to the owning workflow. Synthesize the chosen ideas into one consistent voice and structure, remove repetition, and check that selected claims remain supported. Alternative wording for a specific section is optional when the user requests it.
