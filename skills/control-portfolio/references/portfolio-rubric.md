# Portfolio Review Rubric

## Coverage

- The reporting boundary and as-of time are explicit.
- Every included initiative has a source pointer.
- Missing sources and unavailable systems are named.

## State quality

- Duplicate initiatives are merged only with evidence.
- Owners, milestones, dates, dependencies, and next actions are present or marked missing.
- Stale and conflicting status is visible.
- Done, paused, and cancelled work is not reported as active.

## Intervention quality

- Intervention flags have a concrete trigger and evidence.
- Urgency reflects timing and impact, not rhetorical intensity.
- Recommendations identify who must act and what decision or unblock is needed.
- The executive view contains only items that benefit from the audience's attention.

## Integrity

- Counts derive from the detailed register.
- Tracker writes were authorized, deduplicated, and read back.
- Unknown state remains unknown.

Return `PASS`, `REVISE`, or `BLOCKED`, followed by the smallest changes needed for a trustworthy decision view.
