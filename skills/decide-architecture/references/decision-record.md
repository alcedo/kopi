# Architecture Decision Record

## Decision

`<One sentence naming the chosen direction and scope>`

## Status

Proposed, accepted, superseded, rejected, or under review. Include date, owner, and review condition.

## Problem and outcome

Describe the user, business, and operating outcome. State the decision that must be made.

## Current system and evidence

Summarize observed behavior, ownership, boundaries, data flow, and known constraints. Link every consequential claim. Separate direct evidence, supported inference, and unknowns.

## Constraints and invariants

List security, privacy, reliability, performance, compliance, cost, delivery, compatibility, and organizational constraints that shape the decision.

## Options considered

For each structurally different option, show usage, boundaries, data ownership, operations, failure behavior, migration, cost, strengths, weaknesses, and reversal cost.

## Choice and rationale

Explain why the selected option fits the constraints better. Name accepted tradeoffs and what the team deliberately will not optimize.

## Impact and migration

Identify affected systems, teams, data, interfaces, operations, security controls, and milestones. Sequence migration into reviewable states and define rollback or containment.

## Risks and open questions

Give each material risk an owner, mitigation, trigger, and status. Name evidence that would reverse the decision.

## Verification

State how the design assumptions, implementation, migration, and operating outcome will be checked.
